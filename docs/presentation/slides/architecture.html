AI Support Agent · 4

Three parts. Jira stays the single source of truth: the agent keeps no ticket database of its own. The agent in the middle is a small service we own: Claude does the reading and recommending; our rules, the routing rules in Jira Assets and the approved-fix list decide what happens. On the right: the Claude API, the assignment group set on the ticket, and vendors, who are 3rd-party assignment groups and are already notified by our existing Jira automation. The agent sends no e-mail itself.
