Built-in controls

People first when it matters

Critical or low-confidence tickets go to the triage queue, not the AI.

Personal data redacted

E-mails, phone numbers, card-like numbers and secrets removed before text leaves Jira.

Ticket text is untrusted

Instructions hidden in a ticket are ignored, flagged, and drop it to recommend-only.

Least privilege

One Jira project, read-only Assets, no production systems outside approved fixes.

Full audit trail

Every decision, with its reasoning and confidence, on the ticket and in the logs.

Tested before release

63 automated tests; a backtest against resolved ITSM tickets before going live.

These are the controls built into the code today, not promises for later. The top row protects people and data; the bottom row is about accountability. Worth noting for risk colleagues: the AI never chooses who gets a ticket. It names a service and a category; the routing rules our team keeps in Jira Assets decide the assignment group.
