AI Support Agent · 9 · Proof of concept runs in dry-run mode on a personal account

Where we actually are. The proof of concept runs on a laptop against the ITSM project in dry-run mode, so it reads tickets and shows what it would do, but writes nothing to Jira. It is connected to Jira, Claude and Jira Assets, and it reads the routing model directly from Assets. Today it runs on a personal account and API key; moving it to an approved company account is part of the asks on the last slide. The right-hand list is the next two to four weeks of work, which ends in the backtest numbers that decide whether we go to the live shadow pilot.
