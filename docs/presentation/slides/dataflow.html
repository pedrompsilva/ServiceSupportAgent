Data flow for one ticket

1

A ticket is raised in Jira, MyWorldPanel support.

2

Jira sends only the ticket key to the agent. On a laptop, the agent polls instead.

3

The agent reads the ticket, similar past tickets and the routing rules in Jira Assets.

4

E-mails, phone numbers and secrets are redacted; the text is marked untrusted.

5

Claude returns a structured recommendation: type, severity, service, category, confidence.

6

Our code checks it against the rules and the autonomy level allowed.

7

Allowed actions are applied; the rest are recorded as recommendations.

8

An internal audit comment is posted; the assignment group is recommended or set.

What leaves the company

To Anthropic: redacted ticket text for triage, under our API agreement.

To vendors: nothing new from the agent; our existing Jira automation notifies them.

Nothing else. Jira stays the record; logs keep the decisions for audit.

AI Support Agent · 6 · Steps 1–5 read and recommend · Steps 6–8 decide and act

Walk through left to right, top to bottom. Two points to stress for data protection. First, Jira only sends a ticket key; the agent reads the ticket itself, so nothing in the notification can be spoofed. Second, before any text leaves for Claude, personal data and secrets are stripped out. The agent sends nothing to vendors; existing automation already handles that. The data-handling sign-off from InfoSec is one of the decisions we ask for at the end.
