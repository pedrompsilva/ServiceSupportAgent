What the agent does with each ticket

1

Pick up

A new ticket in MWP support triggers the agent.

2

Triage

Incident, defect or request; severity; affected service and category.

3

Enrich

Finds similar past tickets and how they were solved.

4

Attempt fix

Only pre-approved fixes, such as asking the reporter for missing details.

5

Route

Assignment group from our routing rules in Jira Assets.

6

Update

Audit comment on every ticket; resolves only after a verified fix.

Claude recommends. Our rules decide. People approve each step until the evidence says the agent can do it alone.

AI Support Agent · 3 · Teal = understanding the ticket · Orange = acting on it

Steps 1 to 3 are about understanding the ticket and carry no risk: the agent only reads. Steps 4 to 6 are actions, and each one is switched on separately, per ticket type, once we have evidence it is accurate. The important design point: Claude only recommends. Assignment groups come from the routing rules our team already maintains in Jira Assets, and which fixes may run comes from our own configuration, not from the AI.
